# Finance Friend

Project name idea:

🧠 MoneyGuard AI

“Understand your money. Improve your habits. Make smarter financial decisions.”

🏠 1. Dashboard

Show:

Current balance / sample balance

Monthly spending

Savings

Financial Health Score

Money Goal progress

Spending graph

🎯 2. Money Goals

User sets a goal:

🎓 Education

💻 Laptop

🏠 Future goal

🆘 Emergency savings

AI calculates an educational savings plan based on the target and timeframe.

🤖 3. AI Spending Detector

Upload transaction data/statement.

AI categorizes:

Food 🍔 | Travel 🚌 | Shopping 🛍️ | Bills 📱 | Education 📚 | Other

Then gives insights such as:

“Food spending increased compared with your previous period.”

🔍 4. Hidden Spending Detector

This can be one of your special features.

AI looks for:

Frequent small purchases

Recurring payments

Subscriptions

Unusual increases

Duplicate-looking transactions

Instead of merely showing numbers, it explains the behavior behind the numbers.

📊 5. “What Changed?” Comparison

This is another strong feature.

Previous month vs Current month

AI says:

“Your total spending increased by X%.”

Then breaks down what caused the change.

🧠 6. Financial Health Score

For example:

Financial Health: 74/100

And don't just give a score.

AI explains:

🟢 Good: consistent savings

🟡 Needs attention: recurring expenses

🟠 Watch: discretionary spending

This makes the score educational.

💬 7. “Ask Your Transactions” ⭐

This should definitely be included.

User can type:

“How much did I spend on food?”

“What was my biggest spending category?”

“Which expenses happened repeatedly?”

“Why did my spending increase?”

AI answers using the uploaded transaction data.

That's a good AI feature, rather than a normal dashboard.

📚 8. Financial Literacy Coach

Whenever AI detects a pattern, it teaches the relevant concept.

For example:

AI notices: frequent small purchases

➡️

Mini lesson:

“Small recurring expenses can add up over time.”

➡️

Quick quiz:

“What is a recurring expense?”

This turns your application into a financial education system.

🎮 9. Money Challenge / Gamification

You mentioned animation. This is where you can use it.

For example:

Day 1: Identify needs vs wants

Day 2: Find recurring expenses

Day 3: Create a savings goal

Day 4: Identify a suspicious financial message

Users earn:

🏆 Money Smart Badge

⭐ Savings Streak

🛡️ Scam Awareness Badge

🛡️ 10. Financial Scam Awareness

You can add a section where users paste a suspicious financial message.

AI explains the warning signs and teaches them what to check.

For example:

🚨 Warning signs detected

• Urgency

• Request for sensitive information

• Suspicious payment instructions

Then give a short quiz.

This makes your project much more socially useful.

📈 11. “What If?” Simulator ⭐⭐⭐

This could be your wow feature.

User asks:

“What if I save ₹1,000 every month?”

The application visually shows the hypothetical progress.

Or:

“What happens if this expense category decreases?”

The system shows the projected difference.

This is educational simulation, not a promise of returns.

⚠️ One thing I would CHANGE from your notebook

You wrote something like:

“Give useful accounts to invest & double the money.”

I would NOT present it that way.

“Double your money” sounds like you're promising financial returns, and judges may question the safety and credibility of the application.

Instead make it:

📚 “Investment Literacy”

AI explains concepts such as:

Risk vs return

Diversification

Why guaranteed high returns are a warning sign

Basic financial terminology

How to evaluate financial claims

Don't recommend a specific investment or promise a return.

⭐ The important thing

Don't try to build 12 completely separate applications.

Build one central AI financial engine, and all the features use that same engine.

For tomorrow's hackathon, I'd prioritize:

MVP 🔥

Dashboard

Transaction upload

AI spending analysis

Hidden spending detection

What Changed?

Ask Your Transactions

Financial Health Score

Money Goal

What-If Simulator

Financial literacy/scam mini-quiz

Then the remaining features can be shown as future scope.

That gives you a project that is much more than “AI reads my expenses.” It becomes AI → understands spending → explains behavior → teaches financial literacy → lets the user simulate better choices. 💰🧠
